Carry On Clicker Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Carry On Clicker collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game never connects to any server of ours. Your progress is saved only on your iPhone.

This policy explains what information Carry On Clicker (the iPhone game, "the app") uses and why. The app is published by Cameron Brenneise ("we", "us").

Information stored on your device

To save your game, the app keeps the following on your iPhone:

  • Your game progress, such as Miles, operations, upgrades, eras, prestige, quests, achievements, pilots and themes.
  • Your settings, such as sound, haptics and notification preferences.
  • A record of which in-app purchase rewards have already been added to your game, so they aren't granted twice.

This information stays on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or anyone else, and it is not synced to iCloud. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

In-app purchases

Purchases are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only asks Apple, on your device, which purchases belong to your Apple Account so it can deliver them and restore the First Class Starter Pack. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn them on, reminders (such as a daily reward being ready or your airports waiting) are scheduled on your iPhone itself. They are not sent through any server, and no information leaves your device to create them. You can turn notifications off in the app's Settings or in the iPhone Settings app.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or ask for your name or email.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking tools.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't collect, sell, rent or share any information about you.

Children

Carry On Clicker is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
