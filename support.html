Carry On Clicker Support

Tap, build and prestige your way from a regional airstrip to an airport in every universe.

Need help or have feedback? Email carryonclicker@icloud.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. Progress isn't synced between devices or backed up to a server, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Using Offload App in iPhone Settings keeps your progress.

How do I restore the First Class Starter Pack?

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with, then tap the Boarding Pass counter at the top of the game to open the Store and tap Restore Purchases. The pack and its permanent perks come back. Boarding Pass bundles and Miles Boosters are used up when bought, so Apple doesn't allow them to be restored.

A purchase didn't arrive.

Purchases are added as soon as the App Store confirms them. If a purchase needs approval (for example, Ask to Buy) or was interrupted, it's added the next time you open the game. If something is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

When should I prestige?

Open the Prestige screen and look at "Income after prestige". A good rule of thumb is to prestige when it would roughly double your income. Every prestige point you earn boosts your income forever, and you can spend points on permanent upgrades.

My income suddenly dropped to zero.

That's probably Turbulence Tuesday, a weekly Wormhole Event that pauses passive income in exchange for much more powerful taps. It lasts an hour, and you can end it early from the Wormhole screen.

How do offline earnings work?

Your airports keep earning while the game is closed: at first, up to 2 hours at 25% of your normal rate. Prestige upgrades (Long-Haul Lounge and Autopilot) and the pilot Yuki Tanaka raise the time limit and the rate.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Use the notifications switch in the game's Settings, or go to Settings, Notifications, Carry On Clicker on your iPhone.
